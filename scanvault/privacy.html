Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 7, 2026

ScanVault is a document scanner app. This policy explains what information the app may access and how it is used.

Information We Access

ScanVault may request access to your device camera so you can scan documents.

ScanVault may request access to photos or images you select from your device so you can import them into the app.

Documents, scanned images, and generated PDF files are stored locally on your device unless you choose to share or export them.

Advertising

ScanVault may display ads. Advertising services may collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, app interaction data, and other information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

  • ScanVault does not sell your personal information.
  • ScanVault does not upload your scanned documents to our servers.
  • Third-party services, such as advertising providers, may process data according to their own privacy policies.
