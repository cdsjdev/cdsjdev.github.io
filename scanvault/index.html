Scan, organize, and export documents.

ScanVault is built for everyday paperwork: receipts, forms, notes, records, and other documents you want to keep tidy.

1

Scan with camera

Use your device camera to capture paper documents and turn them into digital pages.

2

Import images

Add selected images from your device when you already have document photos.

3

Organize files

Keep documents grouped in folders so important scans are easier to find later.

4

Export as PDF

Create PDF files for saving, printing, sending, or sharing through other apps.

5

Keep data local

Your scanned documents are stored on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

6

Simple support

For help or privacy questions, contact the developer by email.

Privacy at a glance

ScanVault requests only the access needed for scanning, importing, exporting, and app functionality.

Camera access

Used when you choose to scan a document.

Photo access

Used when you choose to import images from your device.

Local documents

Scans and PDFs stay on your device unless you share or export them.

Support
